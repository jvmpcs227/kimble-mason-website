## Idea 1: "Sandhurst Heritage & Corporate Precision" (Military Heritage meets Corporate Excellence)

* **Design Movement**: Modern Editorial & Military-Corporate Fusion. A blend of classic British institutional prestige and sharp, high-end corporate minimalism.
* **Core Principles**:
  - Authoritative Precision: Grid alignments, clean lines, and meticulous attention to detail.
  - Prestigious Legacy: Incorporating heritage-inspired motifs without feeling dated.
  - Executive Elegance: High-contrast typography and extensive use of purposeful white space.
* **Color Philosophy**: 
  - Deep Sandhurst Navy (`#0A192F` / `oklch(0.18 0.04 250)`) representing trust, stability, and depth.
  - Sovereign Gold (`#D4AF37` / `oklch(0.78 0.12 85)`) representing excellence, prestige, and the elite nature of RMAS.
  - Alabaster White (`#F9F9FB` / `oklch(0.98 0.005 250)`) as the primary light background for ultimate readability.
  - Slate Grey (`#4A5568` / `oklch(0.45 0.02 250)`) for body text and secondary elements.
* **Layout Paradigm**: Asymmetrical split-screen and staggered editorial columns. The hero section features an elegant, dark-navy split screen with a high-contrast portrait on one side and a bold, gold-accented heading on the other, avoiding standard centered grids.
* **Signature Elements**:
  - A subtle "Gold Chevron" divider or thin double-line border representing military rank and structure.
  - Deep-navy textured cards with gold-accented borders for services.
  - Staggered timeline-like list for the qualifications and credentials.
* **Interaction Philosophy**: High-end professional feedback. Buttons scale slightly down on click (tactile press) with smooth hover transitions. Tooltips and dropdowns expand with a crisp, 150ms ease-out curve.
* **Animation**: Minimal and deliberate. Scroll reveals use a very soft fade-in and slide-up (30px) over 250ms with a custom cubic-bezier (`0.23, 1, 0.32, 1`). Absolutely no auto-playing media or rotating carousels.
* **Typography System**:
  - Headings: Playfair Display (Serif) - bringing the prestigious, elite academic and heritage feel of Sandhurst.
  - Body & Subheadings: Plus Jakarta Sans (Sans-Serif) - modern, clean, and highly legible for executive readers.

## Idea 2: "The Strategic Blueprint" (Minimalist Architectural & Structural Design)

* **Design Movement**: Swiss International Typographic Style meets Architectural Minimalism. Focuses on structural integrity, large typography, and functional layouts.
* **Core Principles**:
  - Structure as Ornament: Using borders, grids, and alignment lines as the primary design element.
  - Radical Clarity: Information is presented in a hyper-organized, blueprint-like fashion.
  - Understated Luxury: Relying on exquisite spacing and micro-details rather than decorative graphics.
* **Color Philosophy**:
  - Off-Black / Charcoal (`#121212` / `oklch(0.15 0.01 250)`) for deep contrast.
  - Crisp White (`#FFFFFF` / `oklch(1.0 0.0 0)`) as the structural background.
  - Warm Sand / Desert Gold (`#E6DFD3` / `oklch(0.90 0.02 80)`) as a secondary background and card color, reflecting Western Australia's landscape.
  - Royal Blue (`#1E40AF` / `oklch(0.35 0.15 250)`) as a sharp, modern accent.
* **Layout Paradigm**: Grid-based editorial layout with heavy vertical borders and massive typographic hierarchy. Content flows in clear "zones" divided by fine 1px lines, giving a sense of a structured blueprint or leadership framework.
* **Signature Elements**:
  - Thin 1px structural gridlines separating sections, creating a literal "framework."
  - Large, bold section numbers (e.g., `01`, `02`, `03`) in a monospace font.
  - Clean rectangular cards with hover-induced background color shifts.
* **Interaction Philosophy**: Snappy, direct, and mechanical. Hovering over list items expands them slightly or reveals a subtle arrow indicator.
* **Animation**: Instant transitions or extremely fast linear transitions (100ms). Hover states use immediate color changes. Scroll effects are omitted to respect the "blueprint" aesthetic.
* **Typography System**:
  - Headings: Syne or Space Grotesk (Expressive Sans-Serif) - extremely bold, structural, and modern.
  - Body & Accents: JetBrains Mono or DM Mono (Monospace) for numbers/labels, and Inter for body text.

## Idea 3: "West Coast Executive" (Modern Corporate Coastal Premium)

* **Design Movement**: Premium Editorial / High-End Lifestyle Corporate. Tailored to Perth's elite mining, energy, and corporate sectors.
* **Core Principles**:
  - Premium Sophistication: Soft gradients, glassmorphism, and luxurious textures.
  - Warm Professionalism: Welcoming, emotionally intelligent, yet deeply capable.
  - Local Resonance: Subtle color nods to the Western Australian coast and outback.
* **Color Philosophy**:
  - Ocean Navy (`#0B2545` / `oklch(0.22 0.05 240)`) representing the deep Indian Ocean and executive authority.
  - Sunset Ochre (`#EE964B` / `oklch(0.72 0.14 60)`) as a warm, striking WA-inspired gold/orange accent.
  - Seafoam Mist (`#F4F9F9` / `oklch(0.97 0.01 200)`) as a fresh, modern light background.
  - Soft Charcoal (`#2D3748` / `oklch(0.32 0.02 250)`) for body copy.
* **Layout Paradigm**: Fluid, overlapping card-based layout. Sections overlap and blend into one another with subtle drop shadows and glassmorphic navigation panels.
* **Signature Elements**:
  - Large, high-quality blurred backdrop circles in navy and ochre behind content cards.
  - Floating sticky navigation bar with a heavy backdrop-blur effect.
  - Soft, rounded borders (`rounded-2xl`) and deep, soft shadows to create a multi-layered executive dashboard feel.
* **Interaction Philosophy**: Smooth, fluid, and premium. Everything feels like a well-lubricated high-end dashboard. Hover states fade in smoothly over 200ms.
* **Animation**: Fluid spring animations (`framer-motion` style). Cards slide in with a gentle spring bounce on scroll.
* **Typography System**:
  - Headings: Cormorant Garamond (Elegant Serif) - sophisticated, refined, and highly prestigious.
  - Body: Outift or Satoshi (Modern Sans-Serif) - friendly, highly legible, and premium.
